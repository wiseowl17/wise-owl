Scope: public landing page `/` (Persuade). Visitor: a local small-business owner, usually on a phone, deciding whether this person can build them a real site. Job: believe "he builds real sites" from the four live client sites, then book a call. Proof: barklysclt.com, lighthillstudio.com, eskiconstruction.com, luzreyher.grok.me (screenshots in public/work/). Constraints: PRODUCT.md brand commitments; no prices on this page; no invented claims; primary action "Book a call" opens an on-page call request that saves to the Studio inbox.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a local business directory page, and Wise Owl holds the biggest display ad in the WEB DESIGN category. It refuses the freelancer default of a centered headshot hero over a case-study card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Two inks only: royal blue #1f3fd1 printed on ice white, with a deep navy (a double pass of blue) for body text and a 12% blue tint where ink overlaps. Archivo across widths: expanded black for display ads, condensed for category bands and agate listings. Ruled boxes with square corners, full-width blue category bands with white condensed caps, dot-leader listings, tabular figures, a dashed coupon for the call request. No shadows, no gradients, no paper texture.

STORY: The visitor sees WEB DESIGN, then Wise Owl's ad with a plain promise and Book a call. Below, four client display ads filed under their own trades prove the work. A short how-it-works strip, Manuel's own listing, then the call-request coupon.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Running head with logo and nav. A full-width blue WEB DESIGN band with a see-also line. Under it a three-column directory: Wise Owl's display ad spans two columns (double-ruled box: owl mark and expanded WISE OWL wordmark, headline, one-line subtext, Book a call block set like a bold phone listing, small circular photo with name). The third column is agate: service listings with dot leaders. Book a call is visible without scrolling on desktop and phone.

FORM: The Local Directory, position 3 of 7 on the ordered list, seed key 1bcf03ef. Signature interaction: the call-request coupon prints the sent request back as a new listing line. Motion: one load moment, the category band wiping in and the ad's rules drawing; reduced motion shows everything static.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Unresolved: owner may later supply a booking link (Calendly/Square) to replace the call-request coupon.
