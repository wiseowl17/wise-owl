# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: owners of local small businesses (groomers, contractors, studios, salons, shops, service trades) around Charlotte and beyond who need a site that brings in bookings, calls and payments. They are busy, not technical, often burned by templates or a relative's DIY site, and decide on trust: can I see real work, is he a real person, what will it cost, will he stick around after launch.

Secondary: existing clients returning to the onboarding brief (`/onboard`) or a private rates link (`/p/wise-owl`).

## Product Purpose

Wise Owl is the one-person web design studio of Lorenzo Cruz. The public site exists to turn a small-business owner into an inquiry. Success is a filled contact form or a started onboarding brief.

## Positioning

One person from first call to launch and after: no handoff chain. Seven years of web design, backed by fourteen years of video editing that shows in pacing and what gets cut (background only, not a service). Sites include the working parts a small business needs: booking, payments, calendar sync. Monthly care keeps the owner supported after launch.

## Operating Context

- Contact form and onboarding brief save to the studio database and email the owner.
- Pricing is shared privately via `/p/wise-owl`, never on the public page.
- The owner runs the business from the private Studio (`/login` → `/studio`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Web design only. Never present AI, video, or anything else as a service. Never mention Charlotte as a service line (location is incidental).
- Services: landing pages, small sites (3–5 pages with booking, payments, calendar), larger sites (5+ pages), redesigns, monthly care.
- Public site has no sign-in. Nav "Studio" link goes to `/login`.
- Stack: TanStack Start + React + Tailwind v4, static deploy on Vercel.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Wise Owl. Mark: thin single-line profile owl (`public/logo.svg`), never a cartoon badge.
- Light site: ice-white ground, royal blue accent `#1f3fd1`. Not dark.
- Headshot `public/manuel.jpg` (suit portrait) shown as a modest circle, never full-bleed.
- No paper, desk or laptop imagery.
- Footer copy is exact and centered: "Web Design by Wise Owl." / "Custom sites, built with care." (changed by the owner 2026-09-22; his full name is not in the footer)

## Product Principles

1. Show the work. Real client sites are the strongest argument; lead with them.
2. Plain talk for busy owners: what you get, how it works, how to start.
3. A real person, not an agency costume.
4. Nothing on the page that isn't earning its place.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
